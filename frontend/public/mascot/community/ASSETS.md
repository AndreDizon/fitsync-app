# Community example avatars

- Source: user-supplied `Kawaii Animal Avatar Icon Sticker Sheet.png`.
- Shipped asset: `example-avatars.webp` (1000 × 667, approximately 130 KB).
- The original animal faces and headbands are preserved. CSS round frames crop the six selected profiles from the optimized sheet; the colored avatar discs are retained. This is not an alpha-cutout asset.
- Used only by the fictional Social overview previews, never as real members' profile photos.
- Conversion: Sharp resize to 1000 px wide, WebP quality 82. One shared image supplies all six profiles.

The built-in image tool was tried for background extraction, but its outputs retained background color and were not shipped. Prompt: “Extract the fox, panda, rabbit, tiger, koala and penguin heads in a 3-by-2 transparent grid; preserve faces and headbands; remove discs, glow and background.” A second background-removal pass also retained color. The shipped version uses the supplied artwork directly instead of substituting the generated variants.
